AAC Voice Communication
BLINGS (AAC)

Converts disordered speech into normal speech in real time

PROBLEM & SOLUTION
What it Solves

Patients with speech disorders experience great inconvenience in daily conversation. This solution supports smooth communication by converting disordered speech into clear speech in real time.

KEY FEATURES

Real-time voice conversion (latency less than 0.3 seconds)

Support for various types of speech disorders

Personalized voice profile

Support for mobile apps and wearable devices

Offline mode support

HOW IT WORKS

The AI speech recognition engine converts unclear speech into text, and a personalized TTS model regenerates it into a natural voice. It maximizes clarity while preserving the user's original tone of voice.

Use Cases
01

Communication support for ALS patients

02

03

04

Communication training for children with developmental disabilities

Security & Data Handling

All voice data is processed on the device (on-device AI) and is not transmitted to an external server. It is preparing for medical device standard (FDA, MFDS) certification.
