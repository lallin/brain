AI Music Generation
LUCY - 7

Automatically generates music of the desired genre and mood with a text prompt

PROBLEM & SOLUTION
What it Solves

Video producers and creators spend a lot of time looking for background music that is free of copyright concerns. This solution increases creative efficiency by instantly generating the desired music with just a text description.

KEY FEATURES

Text-based music generation

Fine-tuning of genre, tempo, and instruments

Free length setting from 30 seconds to 5 minutes

Generation of loopable background music

Includes commercial use license

HOW IT WORKS

The Transformer-based music generation model converts text prompts into musical elements (melody, harmony, rhythm). It is automatically arranged according to the user-specified genre and mood, and supports smooth looping by automatically detecting loop points.
