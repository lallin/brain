AI-Powered Zero-Defect Detection for Robot Components
ROBOT COMPONENT DEFECT DETECTION

Real-time detection of micro-defects in robot reducers within 1ms using VIB and Distributional Reinforcement Learning.

PROBLEM & SOLUTION
What it Solves

This solution addresses the limitations of 'random sampling' in the quality validation of Harmonic Drives (Robo Drive), which is often caused by high physical and computational costs. By repurposing high-speed signal integrity technology for mechanical vibration analysis, our 'Digital Stethoscope' AI inspects every single unit on the production line to eliminate tail risks and safety concerns in the collaborative robot (Cobot) market.

KEY FEATURES

VIB(Variational Information Bottleneck) based data compression and ultra-high-speed inspection (51x faster processing)

Worst-Case robustness certification via CVaR (Conditional Value at Risk) optimization for extreme environments

Zero-Shot anomaly detection based on 'Golden Master' data (95.9% detection accuracy for unseen defects)

Automated generation of data-driven 'Safety Certificates' for every individual product

HOW IT WORKS

Our proprietary AI compresses massive sensor data using VIB technology, reducing inspection time per unit to under 1ms. Instead of optimizing for average performance, we use CVaR to mathematically guarantee normal operation even in the bottom 10% worst-case scenarios, utilizing semi-supervised learning to catch even previously unseen defect types.
