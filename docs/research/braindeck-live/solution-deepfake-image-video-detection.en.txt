Deepfake Image/Video Detection
IRIS - 6

Prevents the spread of false information by detecting AI-manipulated images and videos in real time

PROBLEM & SOLUTION
What it Solves

Deepfake videos and images are being abused as a major means of spreading false information on social media and news platforms. This solution secures the reliability of the platform by automatically detecting AI-manipulated content.

KEY FEATURES

Simultaneous detection of image and video deepfakes

Face swap, Face reenactment pattern recognition

Frame-by-frame precision analysis

GPU-accelerated high-volume-processing

Manipulation area visualization report

HOW IT WORKS

The CNN-based video analysis model inspects the face area, boundary artifacts, and temporal consistency for each frame. It determines whether it has been manipulated by analyzing GAN-based generation patterns and compression traces.
