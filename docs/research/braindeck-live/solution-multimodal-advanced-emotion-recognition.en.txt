Multimodal Advanced Emotion Recognition
MULTIMODAL EMOTION RECOGNITION

A precise emotion prediction solution that integrates speech, facial expressions, and biosignals to minimize uncertainty.

PROBLEM & SOLUTION
What it Solves

Relying on a single data source (facial expression or voice alone) often leads to inconsistencies and uncertainty in emotional judgment. This solution utilizes an Evidence Fusion model to process multi-angle sensing data, providing highly reliable emotional state analysis.

KEY FEATURES

Multimodal Data Integration (Speech + Face + Biosignals)

Evidence Fusion Model-based resolution of data inconsistencies

Real-time Emotion Index Visualization (Gauge Dashboard)

Deep Learning-based Audio2Emotion acoustic analysis

Facial Model expression recognition and Biosignal analysis (Heart Rate, EDA, etc.)

HOW IT WORKS

The system concurrently collects voice data from speech audio tracks, facial expression data via cameras, and biosignals (heart rate, blood pressure, EDA) from wearable devices. Each piece of information is processed through an Evidence Fusion model to offset data uncertainties, ultimately delivering precise and high-confidence emotional insights.
